ADR risk — quick FAQ

What does "HFCAA delisting risk" mean?

The Holding Foreign Companies Accountable Act (2020) lets the SEC delist a company after two consecutive years of denied PCAOB audit inspection. PCAOB secured access in Dec 2022, defusing the immediate countdown — but reporting shows political pressure to weaponise delisting re-emerged in 2025–26.

Why is a Hong Kong listing the key mitigant?

If a US listing were forced to close, holders of dual-listed names can usually convert their ADRs into HK shares through their broker. Names with no HK listing (e.g. PDD) have no obvious landing spot, so they carry more exposure.

What is VIE risk, separate from delisting?

Many Chinese firms use a Variable Interest Entity structure — a Cayman holding company with contractual (not ownership) control of the mainland assets. It's a structural/legal risk independent of US-China politics, and applies to most ADRs and ADR-heavy ETFs.

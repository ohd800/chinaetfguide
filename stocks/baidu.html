Where can a foreigner buy Baidu?

You can reach Baidu two ways from outside China: the US-listed ADR BIDU through any normal US brokerage, or the Hong Kong H-share 9888.HK through an international broker with HK access. Because it is dual-listed, an ADR holder has a clear landing spot in Hong Kong if the US line were ever delisted.

ADR delisting risk: Baidu's US line BIDU is rated Medium in our model (the dominant mitigant is its Hong Kong listing status). See the ADR Risk Checker for the full breakdown.

Secondary HK listing alongside the US ADR. Conversion to HK is possible.

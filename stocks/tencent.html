Where can a foreigner buy Tencent?

Tencent trades only in Hong Kong, as the H-share 0700.HK. Buy it through an international broker that offers the HK market (Interactive Brokers, Saxo, and many local platforms).

Common questions

What is Tencent's stock ticker?

It depends on the market: 0700.HK. Tencent trades across multiple markets as listed above.

How do I buy Tencent stock from outside China?

Tencent trades only in Hong Kong, as the H-share 0700.HK. Buy it through an international broker that offers the HK market (Interactive Brokers, Saxo, and many local platforms).

Why is there no Tencent ADR?

Tencent has no US-listed ADR. Foreigners reach it via its H-share instead.

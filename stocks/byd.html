Where can a foreigner buy BYD?

BYD trades only in Hong Kong, as the H-share 1211.HK. Buy it through an international broker that offers the HK market (Interactive Brokers, Saxo, and many local platforms).

Rare double listing: mainland A-share (Shenzhen) AND Hong Kong H-share. No US ADR currently. Foreigners usually reach it via the H-share.

Common questions

What is BYD's stock ticker?

It depends on the market: 002594.SZ / 1211.HK. BYD trades across multiple markets as listed above.

How do I buy BYD stock from outside China?

BYD trades only in Hong Kong, as the H-share 1211.HK. Buy it through an international broker that offers the HK market (Interactive Brokers, Saxo, and many local platforms).

Why is there no BYD ADR?

BYD has no US-listed ADR. Foreigners reach it via its H-share instead.

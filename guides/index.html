How to invest from your country

From the United States →US brokerages list China ETFs and ADRs directly. From Europe (EU) →Use a UCITS China ETF — PRIIPs blocks US funds for retail. From the United Kingdom →UCITS China ETF for retail; ADRs and H-shares are open. From Canada →Brokers with US access open ETFs and ADRs. From Australia →US access or ASX-listed China ETFs in AUD. From Singapore →Easy HKEX + US access; Stock Connect for A-shares. From Hong Kong →The gateway: direct HKEX and Stock Connect Northbound. From other countries →A global broker with US + HK access covers most routes.
